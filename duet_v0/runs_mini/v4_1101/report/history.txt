Duet v4 history ablation in music_nd (PROGRAM §5.5): how much of her past melody does the real-time chord guess need?
Mac mini run v4_1101, Phase D, 2026-11-01.  val only, tuned on val (this bundle has no test split).

music_nd = share of her singing time (from beat 4) where Duet is consonant with her AND on a tone of the true chord AND not her note/octave;
headline = mean of bach (17 val songs), folk (170), pop (300), pop909 (40).  double = share of time Duet sings her note or its octave.
H = how many beats of her past melody the chord guesser may use.  Only the chord guess is limited; the chooser is unchanged and always
remembers Duet's own previous note for voice leading (that is Duet's line, not her melody).
  HMM: the filter is re-run over beats k-H .. k-1 plus the current beat k, starting from the song-start chord prior; H=0 = the current beat only
       (her current note plus any earlier note inside the same beat).
  GRU: each of the 3 GRUs is re-run from an empty state over the notes that started in the last H beats; H=0 = her current note only.
  whole song = the unchanged systems (running HMM filter; GRU hidden state carried through the song).
Files: m3_seq_v5h0/h4/h16 are the legacy wrappers that set DUET_HIST inside their own process (the outside environment was empty);
rD_hist_m1_h*_v1 and rD_hist_mix_h*_v1 are new constant-only wrappers (filter.H = H; GRU ensemble built with hist = H). Scored with
rescore_nd (runs_mini/v4_1101/nd/<name>/) and bench3 (one leaderboard row each).

What it shows:
- HMM (m1_filter_v5lo): her past melody is worth only +0.0105, almost all of it inside the last 4 beats. With 16 beats the scores equal the whole-song filter's
  (music_nd and double identical to every digit; bench3 rows identical except folk leap 3.6051 vs 3.6053):
  the filter forgets notes older than about 16 beats on its own. pop gains nothing from history.
- With no history the champion is no better than the HMM (H=0: 0.6114 vs 0.6101). Its whole gain over m1_filter_v5lo (+0.0238)
  comes from the GRUs remembering more of her melody (champion minus HMM at the same window: +0.0013 at H=0, +0.0141 at 4 beats, +0.0216 at 16 beats, +0.0238 for the whole song).
- m3_seq_v5 gains +0.0337 in music_nd, but on bach it LOSES with more history (0.6432 -> 0.6241): the surer its chord guess, the more its own chooser
  copies her (bach double 0.077 -> 0.184), and music_nd counts copying as a miss.
