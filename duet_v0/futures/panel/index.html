Duet, beat by beat

The original Duet: on every beat it guesses ahead and sings one harmony note in its own voice. It needs the key and the beat: either it follows a count-in played from the phone, or it gives the click and the singer follows.

The key is the one set in the Live harmonizer card (the built-in singer is always C major). With the count-in the tempo comes from the phone's clicks. Test clips with a count-in: Twinkle (C major, 80 BPM) and the vocadito singer (A major, about 122 BPM).
